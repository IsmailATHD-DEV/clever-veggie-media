Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who uses the tool

clever.veggie studio is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @clever.veggie. It has no other users and collects no data about TikTok viewers.

What data is handled

  • TikTok authorisation: when the owner connects their own TikTok account, TikTok returns an access token and basic account information (scope user.info.basic). The token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload the owner's carousels to their own TikTok inbox (scope video.upload).
  • Slides: finished carousel images are published on this website so TikTok can download them. They contain no personal data.
  • Statistics: with the owner's permission (scopes user.info.stats and video.list), the tool reads the owner's own follower count and the public statistics of the owner's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares), and stores them only on the owner's computer to measure what works. The owner may also type these statistics in manually.

What is not done

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or collected from anyone other than the owner. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored token from their computer.
