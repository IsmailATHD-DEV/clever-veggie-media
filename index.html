clever.veggie studio

clever.veggie studio is a personal tool used by the owner of the TikTok account @clever.veggie. It prepares photo carousels about history, inventions and everyday life in the past, with every fact checked against two sources and every image openly licensed.

When a carousel is ready, the tool sends it to the owner's own TikTok inbox through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The owner then chooses the sound and publishes it in the TikTok app.

This site only hosts the finished slides and the pages below. There is no public sign-up and no other users.
